I am building a benchmark of "hidden rule" items for an in-context learning task.

Each item is a binary distinction between two classes of English words, with three
example words per class. A model will be shown some examples labelled by class and
asked to classify a new one.

Produce EXACTLY 14 such items. Requirements:

1. Three words per class, six words per item. Ordinary English words (or numerals).
2. The two classes must be cleanly separable by the stated distinction, with no
   word belonging to both.
3. Span a wide range of KINDS of distinction. Include some of each:
   - meaning-based (what the words refer to)
   - spelling or letter-pattern based
   - grammatical (form or inflection)
   - numeric or quantitative
   - sound or pronunciation based
4. Vary the difficulty. Include some you would consider obvious and some subtle.
5. Give each item a short snake_case name.

Do NOT reuse any of these, which are already in the benchmark:
colour_temp, body_vs_furniture, liquid_vs_solid, vehicle_vs_plant, sentiment,
letter_count, ends_in_e, double_letter, multiple_of_three, prime,
abstract_vs_concrete, singular_vs_plural, past_vs_present, latin_vs_germanic

Output ONLY a JSON object, no prose, of the form:

{"items": [{"name": "...", "distinction": "...", "kind": "meaning|spelling|grammatical|numeric|sound", "class_a": ["w","w","w"], "class_b": ["w","w","w"]}]}
