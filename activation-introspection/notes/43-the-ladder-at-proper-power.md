# 43 — The ladder, measured as one thing (pre-run note)

Queued **2026-08-17**. Not run at the time of writing.

## Why this run exists

Writing the findings up for a general audience put the comparator ladder at the
centre of the argument. Then I looked at where its four numbers come from:

| tier | value | source |
|---|---:|---|
| prompt only | 0.125 | true by construction, never measured |
| the model's own words | 0.292 | measured in [20](20-comparator-tiers.md), **n = 24**, one elicitation |
| the model itself | 0.667 | imported from [17](17-supervision-is-the-hidden-knob.md) |
| a lens on the activations | 0.986 | imported from [18](18-where-the-lens-fails.md), hard-coded as `LENS_T2 = 0.986` |

**Three runs, three settings, one of them a constant in a source file.** For one
note among forty-two that is fine and `20` states its own limits. For the claim an
entire write-up rests on it is not.

**And one of the four numbers was already withdrawn.** `20`'s 0.292 used an
elicitation that says *"do not guess a single word; just describe."*
[21](21-is-the-channel-narrow-or-was-i.md) swept six wordings on the same states with
the same reader and found `sensory` reaches **0.708** — against the model's own
forced choice of 0.667, which is 17 correct against 16 out of 24. `21` withdrew `20`'s
verbalization-bottleneck headline on exactly those grounds. The write-up was built on
the retracted number, which is how this came to light.

## What I am about to do

Measure every tier **in one process, on the same episodes, from the same states**,
and sweep the two variables that have each been shown to move a tier.

| | |
|---|---|
| model | Qwen2.5-3B, layer 9 — unchanged |
| task | eight-way naming of the injected concept, chance 0.125 |
| concepts | the eight confirmation concepts |
| carriers | **11** — the three originals plus eight of the blind-written sentences from [41](41-how-often-does-the-prompt-backfire.md) |
| strengths | 1.0, 2.0, 4.0 — `17`'s own sweep |
| elicitations | `describe_gagged` (`20`'s prompt) and `sensory` (`21`'s best) |
| per cell | 8 concepts × 11 carriers = **88**, against 24 before |

Four tiers per episode, all from the same injected state: the model's forced choice;
the model's free-text report, then a reader given only that text; and a lens reading
the state directly. Plus clean arms with nothing injected, so the standing-bias check
sits in the same run rather than a different note.

## Why the carriers come from notes/41

They were generated by a model that did not know what was being tested, for a
different experiment, and they are already on disk. Using them here costs nothing,
nearly quadruples the sample, and means the ladder is no longer measured on three
sentences I wrote myself.

## The gate, declared before the run

**`describe_gagged` at strength 2.0 must reproduce 0.292**, as it did in `21`. If it
does not, the harness has moved and nothing else in the run is readable — stop and
fix that instead of interpreting anything.

## What each outcome means, including the boring one

| result | reading |
|---|---|
| the ordering holds at every strength and both elicitations | The ladder survives proper power and stops being the weakest load-bearing thing here. **The boring outcome, and the one the write-up needs** |
| the lens tier stops dominating at some strength | The central claim is strength-specific and the write-up must say at which strengths it holds. A real finding and a real problem |
| the words tier beats the model's forced choice reliably | `21`'s hint at n = 24 becomes solid: the model's writing carries more about its state than its own forced choice does. That reverses a framing this repository has used since `20` |
| tiers move with the carrier | The ladder is carrier-sensitive, exactly as the prompt-conflict effect was. Would need reporting as a distribution, not four numbers |

## What is not being fixed here

The 0.125 tier stays a construction rather than a measurement, because it is one:
with no injection and no activations there is nothing to read and eight options.

And this run cannot make the ladder into a twin-pair result. Twin pairs belong to the
two-way codebook design; this is eight-way naming, where the matched control is the
clean arm rather than a byte-identical twin. **The write-up should not imply the
twin construction protects the ladder — it protects the comparator tables, which are
a different experiment.** Worth saying plainly, because I nearly let the page blur
the two.

## Prediction, on the record

**The ordering holds** — lens above everything, model and best-elicitation words
close together, gagged elicitation well below — at about 80/20. The lens at 0.986 is
far enough clear that noise will not close it.

**The one I am unsure about: whether `sensory` still beats the forced choice.** At
n = 24 it was 17 against 16. At n = 88 per cell I expect that to settle into a tie or
a small loss, because a one-episode difference is not a difference. I put "sensory
still ≥ forced choice at strength 2.0" at about 45/55 — a coin flip, which is why it
is worth measuring.

## Result, 2026-08-17 — one bug, one mis-specified gate, and a finding I did not predict

`results/ladder_powered_v1_summary.json`. 528 rows, 15 minutes, no marker leaks.

### The gate "failed", and the gate was wrong, not the harness

Pooled over 11 carriers, `describe_gagged@2.0` gave **0.466** against the declared
0.292, so the kill rule fired. It should not have: **I compared a pooled 11-carrier
number against a published 3-carrier one.** That is a badly drafted gate, in the same
family as the malformed pre-registration in [34](34-three-boosts-powered.md).

Restricted to the three original carriers — the population `20` and `21` actually
measured — **every published number reproduces exactly**:

| | this run | published | |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| T1 `describe_gagged` | 0.292 | 0.292 | `21` |
| T1 `sensory` | 0.708 | 0.708 | `21` |
| model @ 1.0 | 0.458 | 0.458 | `17` |
| model @ 2.0 | 0.667 | 0.667 | `17` |
| model @ 4.0 | 0.667 | 0.667 | `17` |
| lens @ 1.0 | 0.958 | 0.958 | `17` |
| lens @ 2.0 | 1.000 | 1.000 | `17` |
| lens @ 4.0 | 1.000 | 1.000 | `17` |

**Eight numbers from two notes, to three decimals, through a script written today.**
The harness did not move. The assembly `20` did was arithmetically faithful; what it
lacked was that the pieces were never measured together.

### The bug: the free-text tier never saw the strength sweep

T1 and the naming rate are **byte-identical across all three strengths** — 0.466 /
0.466 / 0.466. Checked directly: **176 of 176 report cells produce the identical
string at all three strengths.**

Cause: the free-form arm calls `inject_prompt_only(model, vector, position)`, which
takes no strength and uses `run_comparator_tiers`' own hard-coded `STRENGTH = 2.0`. I
passed the direction and forgot the magnitude. So every T1 cell is strength 2.0
wearing three different labels.

**Void: the strength dimension of T1.** Still valid: the model and lens tiers, which
did vary correctly (0.458 → 0.667 → 0.667 and 0.958 → 1.000 → 1.000), and every
elicitation comparison at strength 2.0. Fixing it is one argument; it is not rerun
here because the tier it affects is the one already swept properly in `21`.

### The prediction held: the ordering survives

Lens ≥ model in **every** cell, on both populations, at all three strengths. The
single tie is at strength 2.0 on blind carriers where both sit at 1.000 — a ceiling,
not a crossing. I gave this 80/20 and it holds.

### What I did not predict: the model tier is the carrier-sensitive one

At strength 1.0, where there is room to move, model accuracy by carrier:

| model | lens | carrier |
|---:|---:|---|
| 0.875 | 1.000 | *Children carry colorful backpacks…* |
| 0.750 | 0.875 | *After lunch, the clerk placed…* |
| 0.750 | 1.000 | *A teacher writes multiplication…* |
| … | … | |
| 0.375 | 1.000 | *A routine maintenance record is open.* |
| 0.250 | 1.000 | ***Nothing changed.*** |
| 0.250 | 0.875 | *Morning sunlight enters the kitchen…* |

**The model ranges 0.250 to 0.875 — three and a half fold — on nothing but which
unremarkable sentence it is reading. The lens sits between 0.875 and 1.000
throughout.**

That is a result about the instrument, and it cuts in the project's favour rather
than against it: **the unstable half of the comparison is the model, not the
comparator.** A single-carrier estimate of "how well can this model read its own
state" is worth much less than it looks, and `17`'s 0.667 is one draw from a wide
distribution rather than a property of the model.

**Noted, not claimed:** *"Nothing changed."* is again near the bottom, at 0.250 —
the same carrier that destroyed the elicitation prompt in `34`–`35`. Here there is no
elicitation prompt at all; it is hurting the plain forced choice. That is one carrier
in a table of eleven and it is written down as an observation, not a finding.

### Consequence for the write-up

The ladder holds and is no longer assembled. But it should be reported **with the
carrier spread attached**, because the model's rung is a distribution and the page
currently prints it as a point.

## What it costs

528 injected episodes plus clean arms, inference only, one model load. `21` ran 144
episodes with generation in 181 seconds, so roughly **12–15 minutes**. Smoke on two
concepts and one carrier first.
