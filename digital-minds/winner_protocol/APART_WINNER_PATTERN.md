# Apart Winner Pattern — Inferred, Not an Official Rubric

Recent Apart winner pages suggest a practical pattern.

## What judges explicitly praised

- **clear conceptualization and operationalization**;
- **surprising quantitative results**;
- readable presentation;
- concrete mechanism or intervention, not only measurement;
- breadth when it remains coherent;
- controls/validation;
- an artifact worth continuing after the weekend.

## What judges explicitly wanted more of

- formal statistical testing;
- repeated evaluations / variance checks;
- clearer configuration details;
- false-positive analysis;
- larger or more realistic samples;
- clearer scope where a method could be overfit.

## Implication for this project

Do not optimize for “most experiments.”

Optimize for:

1. one clean causal contrast;
2. one surprising dissociation or failure;
3. a control that defeats the obvious alternative explanation;
4. enough independent carrier units for intervals;
5. transparent frozen configuration;
6. two readable figures;
7. a narrow claim that obviously admits continuation into a paper.

The visible X/Y persona capability control is especially valuable under this
inferred rubric because it turns a potential reviewer objection into a plotted
diagnostic rather than a paragraph of speculation.
